## Design Context

### Users
Moses (the operator) and, through him, three situations: watching what the agent does inside an iOS app while chatting; driving the app by hand as a QA operator with device picker, hardware buttons, and keyboard; showing the running app to a client. The simulator tab lives inside the Moses AI Web GUI beside Chat and Trajectory and must hold up in all three without a mode switch.

### Brand Personality
Instrumental, exact, quiet. The panel is an apparatus on a bench: the device is the object, the chrome is the bench. No decoration, no marketing surface, no glow. Copy is short and factual (ru primary, en, zh).

### Aesthetic Direction
Light application chrome from the Moses AI theme tokens (`--dsw-alias-*`, `--dsw-font-family`); the stage the phone sits on is a deep tinted neutral so the raster reads at full contrast in every state. Controls compress to one instrument strip; device inventory and device creation live in a popover, never as a permanent list. Anti-references: native `<select>` and unstyled buttons; dashboards with cards inside cards; dark mode with neon accents.

### Design Principles
- The screen gets the area: everything else earns its pixels or folds away.
- State is always readable at a glance: which device, whether the stream is live, what failed.
- Reuse the app's primitives (`Button`, `Pill`, `Menu`, `Input`, `StateDot`) and tokens; the tab must feel native to Moses AI.
- Hardware controls sit where the hardware is: beside the device, as keycaps, not as a toolbar.
- Every state is designed: empty, connecting, live, stopped, error, no inventory.
